More possibilities: processing recipes

Switch / reed / PIR: polarity, stable-time debounce, rising/falling events and occupancy. Photodiode: analog front-end, median/EMA cleanup, separate LOW/HIGH thresholds; bandpass and optical demodulation next. Pulse / encoder: timed edges, pulse widths, duty, frequency and quadrature decoding need dedicated capture support.

Images: camera channels carry frames, not bit strings. Background subtraction, thermal calibration, connected regions and Canny/optical flow belong to negotiated camera/host pipelines. Audio: gain, filtering, spectrum, formants and event detection are separate derived channels. The processing catalog explains which steps can run on-device and which are still planned.

What the markers mean

Centers are local maxima of a 2 ms liftered cepstral envelope. Bandwidth edges are that envelope's measured −3 dB crossings within each search range, not LPC pole bandwidths. Unresolved edges are omitted.

The cepstrum axis is milliseconds. Its formant readout is linked to the frequency view; no reciprocal-frequency formant markers are drawn.
